# Presentation reports

Standalone pages (open in a browser) built from real data on 2026-11-08.

- `data-volume.html`: how much data the live pipeline pulls and how often (requests/day, gauge readings, radar grids, USGS reporting delay, the July 4, 2025 timeline, the 15 sources evaluated).
- `sensor-gaps.html`: where public flood sensing runs out, the case for drones: Texas counties with no real-time gauge, gauge drainage areas, Flash Flood Alley coverage, ungauged crossings in the Kerr County region, radar beam height over Kerrville and Hunt, and the Hunt gauge going silent at the July 4 crest.

Sources: USGS Water Services site registry (TX, parameter 00065), U.S. Census 2024 Gazetteer, NEXRAD KEWX geometry, USGS 3DEP, the project's Kerr County road model, and approved USGS readings for July 4, 2025.
