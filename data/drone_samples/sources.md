# Drone sample sources

All images come from Wikimedia Commons. Each license was checked against the license template in the file page's wikitext (Flickr imports carry a Flickr review). The images were downscaled to at most 1280 px wide and re-encoded as JPEG with PIL; nothing else was edited. CC BY images require attribution to the author when shown.

Note: these are ground-level and elevated oblique photos, not true drone footage. Top-down aerials on Commons did not show cars clearly enough to use.
