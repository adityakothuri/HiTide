# Mobile App

Consumer phone app in `mobile/`: Expo SDK 58, Expo Router, TypeScript. The design ground truth is `design/mockups/2026-11-08/consumer-v2/CLAUDE-CODE-PROMPT.md`, with its two image boards: white and royal blue, Home / Trips / Updates / Profile, focused turn-around, check-in and support flows.

## Two data modes, one boundary

Screens only use `useAppState()` and `useRepository()` (`mobile/src/data/RepositoryProvider.tsx`). `HiTideRepository` has two implementations:

- **Live** (`mobile/src/data/live/`) is used when `EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL` is set. It follows [[Per-User Danger]] for one user, `hc-dana` (Dana Whitfield, sim-only phones, generated by `backend/scripts/gen_region_users.py` after the seeded 250).
- **Sample** (`mobile/src/data/mockRepository.ts`) uses local fixtures and needs no backend.

## Live pipeline

1. A ping (demo drive) goes to `POST /api/users/hc-dana/location`. The backend assesses hazard, vulnerability and motion, then runs the ladder.
2. The app polls `GET /danger` every 5 s. A `user.danger` WS event triggers an immediate refetch.
3. The payload is validated with zod (`schema.ts`). `mapAssessment.ts` then maps it onto app state as a pure function with tests:
   - warning, closure, and turn-around (once per flooded road)
   - check-in on `awaiting_reply` or `case_open`
   - support when `need_help`, with the timeline built from the backend's own events
4. Replies go to `POST /reply`. A route choice goes to `POST /route`, the pre-trip check.

## Truth rules kept

- Unknown depth reads "depth not measured", never dry.
- No route is called safe.
- NWS issue and expiry times are left blank when the assessment doesn't carry them. The card shows "checked <time>" instead.
- Outreach in the replay is labelled simulated.
- The header badge always says where the data comes from: Live · July 4, 2025 replay, Connecting, Offline, or Sample data.

## Bugs caught by the review loop

- The danger ladder's SMS poll arrives while Dana is still driving. A check-in keyed on `awaiting_reply` alone opened "Are you safe?" at 30 mph. The check-in now requires a non-driving state.
- Metro's watcher (a watchman recrawl) served a stale bundle. Restart with `npx expo start --clear` if behaviour doesn't match the code.

## Flood map, route choices, assistant line (2026-11-08)

- **Flood layers:** `GET /api/region/flood-layer` gives the non-open roads with geometry, crossings, gauges and alerts for one window. Drawn in the dashboard palette, but quieter:
  - likely flooded: thin translucent amber, with no dashes, which turned short segments into blobs
  - unknown: a faint hairline
  - crossings: dots only where there is water
- **Route choices:** `GET /api/routes/options` returns the fastest route plus two that avoid confirmed flooding. Options are compared by road, because both directions of a road are parallel edges.
- **Directions to higher ground:** `GET /api/users/{id}/directions` reuses the region's `route_out`. Someone stopped on a flooded road is told to *leave* it, never to go "by" it.
- **Assistant line:** `POST /api/users/{id}/assistant` writes the context into the agent's default variables, returns the number, and watches the agent's inbound conversations.
  - A "need help" outcome takes the existing call-result path and flags responders.
  - One session at a time; defaults are reset to neutral text afterwards.
  - Each conversation is claimed once, inbound only.
  - Gated by `HITIDE_ASSISTANT_LIVE=1`. Prompt and setup are in `docs/voice-agent.md` §8.
- **Not verified:** a real phone call to the assistant. It needs a person and the live flag.

## Demo tip

`POST /api/demo/start?scenario=hill_country&speed=1000` reaches the Kerrville crest in about a minute, and the replay then holds its final state. That gives a repeatable flooded picture for the demo drive. Restarting the replay also resets Dana's episode.
